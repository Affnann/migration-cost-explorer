About the fee caps. The government sets a maximum amount workers can be charged for some countries. The caps shown here are the figures reported in Bangladeshi newspapers (most from the government's 2017 revision, Malaysia from 2022). The official notices were not found online, so these may be out of date. Check with the Probashbondhu call centre (16135) before relying on them.
